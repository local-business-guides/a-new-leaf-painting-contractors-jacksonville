JACKSONVILLE, Florida (November 13, 2026) A New Leaf Painting Contractors published a guide for Jacksonville homeowners considering house painters, along with a podcast episode and an audio version on SoundCloud.

The guide offers practical points for homeowners to consider when choosing a painting contractor. It gives readers a place to start before discussing the work they need, comparing options or requesting an estimate. That can be useful for projects that involve more than applying paint, such as drywall or stucco repairs, wood rot, carpentry or cabinet work.

A New Leaf Painting Contractors provides interior and exterior painting, cabinet painting, commercial painting, drywall and stucco repair, wood rot and carpentry repairs, pressure washing, and garage floor coatings. The company serves Jacksonville, Ponte Vedra Beach, Nocatee, St. Johns, Fleming Island, Orange Park and St. Augustine. The guide may help area homeowners think through their needs before reaching out to a contractor.

The guide and podcast episode were published this week. The audio version is on SoundCloud. No direct link to the guide or podcast episode was included in the publication information.

A New Leaf Painting Contractors has served Northeast Florida since 2010, and the owner’s painting experience dates to 1988. The insured company uses Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore coatings, provides written estimates and a written warranty, and reports more than 700 five-star reviews across Google and other platforms. Homeowners can schedule a free estimate.
