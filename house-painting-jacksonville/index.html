What to include in your project checklist

Painting involves more than applying a new color. Preparation and any needed repairs should be considered as part of the plan. Depending on the surfaces and scope, work may include:

  • Interior painting or exterior painting
  • Cabinet painting
  • Drywall or stucco repair
  • Wood rot and carpentry repairs
  • Pressure washing or garage floor coatings

Before work starts, move small belongings away from the areas being painted and tell the estimator about access needs or surfaces you want left untouched. Avoid choosing a painter based only on a broad verbal description. Ask what areas are included, what preparation is expected, and whether repairs are part of the written estimate. If you find damaged drywall, stucco, or wood, bring it up rather than assuming paint alone will address it.
