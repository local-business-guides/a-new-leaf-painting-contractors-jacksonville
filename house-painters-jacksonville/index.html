What to check before work begins

A home painting project can cover interior or exterior painting, cabinets, and related surface work. We also handle drywall and stucco repair, wood rot and carpentry repairs, pressure washing, and garage floor coatings. The right scope depends on what you want done and what the surfaces need. Before the scheduled work, use this checklist:

  • Confirm which rooms, surfaces, and trim are included.
  • Point out previous patches, water marks, peeling areas, or damaged wood.
  • Remove small breakable items from the work area and identify belongings that need extra protection.
  • Discuss the coating and finish you have in mind, along with any questions about the estimate.

One common mistake is choosing a paint color before identifying a repair that could change the finished appearance. Another is assuming that every painter includes the same surface preparation. Ask about those details before approving the scope. If you find soft or rotted wood, damaged stucco, or drywall cracks that keep returning, bring them up rather than treating them as paint-only issues.
